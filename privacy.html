Playing without an account

You can play without signing up. Your photos, finished puzzles, saved game and daily streak then stay in your own browser (its local storage) and are never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Technical data

Like every website, our server sees your IP address when you visit. We use it only to protect the site: to slow down repeated failed sign-ins and to keep the service running. Failed-attempt counters are kept in memory for 15 minutes. Our hosting provider keeps short technical logs (time, address, page) for security and troubleshooting.

Fonts and all other files are served from our own site; your browser doesn't contact other companies when you visit.

Why we may use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): your account, gallery, saved games, friends and games.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): keeping the site secure, preventing abuse and handling reports.

Who helps us run it

We use a few service providers who process data only on our behalf, under data processing agreements:

  • Render (Render Services, Inc.) runs our server, in its Frankfurt (EU) region.
  • Neon (Neon, Inc.) hosts our database, in its Frankfurt (EU) region.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) sends our emails.

Render and Neon are US companies. Where data could be accessed from outside the EU, they rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

We never sell your data or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your profile, gallery, saved game, achievements, streak, friends and games from our database straight away.
  • Photos picked for a game with a friend: deleted when the game ends (or when the invite expires).
  • Unconfirmed email links: 48 hours. Password reset links: 30 minutes.
  • Reports: as long as needed to deal with them. If you delete your account, reports you made no longer point to you.
  • Our database provider keeps short-term backups for recovery; deleted data disappears from them within days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download, restrict or delete your data, and object to how we use it. Most of this you can do yourself in your account: change your email or password, download your data, or delete your account. For anything else, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Children

Puzzle Table is for everyone, but accounts are meant for people aged 16 or older. Younger players need a parent's or guardian's permission to create one, or can simply play without an account.

Security

The site only works over an encrypted connection. Passwords are hashed, sign-in cookies are protected against other sites, attempts are rate-limited, and every player can only ever reach their own data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes we'll tell you in the app or by email.
